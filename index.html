We often overlook the small things that surround us every day, like the standpipes scattered throughout our streets. Yet these seemingly ordinary objects are part of what shapes a city, each contributing to its character, color, and identity.

Standpipe Olympics brings together standpipes from cities around the world, celebrating the unique forms, details, and personalities hidden within a shared function.
